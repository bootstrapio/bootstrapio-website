The Mermaid Club and Amenities

Your Own Private Waterfront

At Continuum 12000, residents enjoy ~150,000 SQ FT of indoor and outdoor amenities. On the water is The Mermaid Club, a 1.5 acre private waterfront club on Biscayne Bay, with a sun deck, floating pool, yoga deck, paddleboards, jet skis, and a marina for yachts up to 90 feet. Inside, residents have access to a collection of fitness and wellness spaces including a full spa with cold plunge and sauna, indoor sports courts, golf and F1 simulators, outdoor dining and a Longevity Lounge featuring red light, recovery and biohacking tools.
