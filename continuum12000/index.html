The Residences

Room to Live Well

Continuum 12000 offers one- to three-bedroom residences ranging from 1,035 to 3,543 square feet, featuring floor-to-ceiling glass windows, ceiling heights of at least 10 feet, expansive balconies, and sweeping bay views. The Mermaid Club and Amenities

Your Own Private Waterfront

At Continuum 12000, residents enjoy approximately 150,000 square feet of world-class indoor and outdoor sport, wellness, and leisure amenities. At the heart of the experience is The Mermaid Club™, a private waterfront destination extending both on and above Biscayne Bay, complemented by 1.5 acres of waterfront recreation. Amenities include a sun deck, floating pool, yoga deck, paddleboards, jet skis, and a marina accommodating yachts up to 90 feet. Additional offerings include a full-service spa with cold plunge and sauna, indoor sports courts, golf and F1 simulators, outdoor dining, and a Longevity Lounge featuring red light therapy, recovery treatments, and biohacking technologies.
